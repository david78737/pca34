# Landing a PM Role in 2026
**Tyler Mulvenna, Edwin Sequeira, Jeremy Bagley, Justin Hunter** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
This hybrid presentation and panel tackles the realities of the 2026 PM job market with both structured frameworks and candid panelist experience. The session covers automation workflows for job discovery and networking, giving attendees practical tools alongside strategic career advice. For anyone actively job searching or preparing for a future PM role search, this is the most directly actionable session of the conference.

## Key Takeaways
- The 2026 PM job market has specific dynamics — AI automation, portfolio expectations, and hiring signals — that require updated search strategies
- Automation workflows for job discovery and networking can dramatically increase the quality and volume of relevant opportunities
- Panel perspectives from practitioners who have recently hired or been hired provide ground-truth calibration that job search advice rarely includes

## Who Should Read This
Aspiring PMs, PMs between roles, and PMs considering a move who want a current, practical guide to navigating the 2026 hiring landscape.

## The Brief
Update your job search strategy before your next application — the tools and signals that worked in 2023 are not the ones that will get you hired in 2026.
