# Monetize What You Know: AI-Powered Systems for Courses, Templates, and Automation Workflows
**Chris Lopez** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Lopez provides a framework for turning professional expertise into digital revenue streams — courses, templates, and automation workflows — using a modern AI toolstack including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Notion, and Make.com. The workshop is built for practitioners who already have valuable knowledge and need a system to package and sell it. For product professionals eyeing entrepreneurial income streams, this is a practical starting point.

## Key Takeaways
- Professional expertise can be systematically transformed into scalable digital products with the right AI-assisted workflow
- Tools like Notion and Make.com enable automation of the creation, distribution, and sales pipeline without engineering resources
- The monetization barrier for knowledge products is lower than ever — the bottleneck is system design, not content creation

## Who Should Read This
Product managers, marketers, and consultants who want to build side income or transition to entrepreneurship by productizing their existing professional knowledge.

## The Brief
Identify one piece of expertise you've repeatedly shared for free and use this framework to outline a digital product you could sell within 30 days.
