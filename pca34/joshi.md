# Elevate Your API Game — Bring the Swagger Back
**Adwait Joshi** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Joshi walks through the fundamentals of building robust API foundations, from platform orchestration to enabling third-party integrations. The session culminates in preparing your stack for AI with Model Context Protocol (MCP), positioning APIs as a strategic lever rather than a backend afterthought. For PMs who manage platform or integration-heavy products, this is essential architectural context.

## Key Takeaways
- Solid API design is the foundation for scalable platform ecosystems and partner integrations
- Orchestrating platforms well unlocks compounding value through third-party extensibility
- MCP is an emerging standard for making your stack AI-ready — understand it before your engineers have to explain it to you

## Who Should Read This
Product managers working on platforms, APIs, or developer-facing products who need to speak fluently with engineering about integration architecture and AI readiness.

## The Brief
Treat your API strategy as a product strategy — invest in the foundations now so your platform is ready for the AI integrations your customers will demand next.
