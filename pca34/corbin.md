# Claude for Product Managers: Automate the Work. Elevate Your Impact
**Dan Corbin, Pavan Valluri** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Corbin and Valluri demonstrate how Claude Desktop, Cowork, Skills, and Code can be integrated into core PM workflows to automate routine tasks and accelerate analysis across discovery, planning, and execution. The session is grounded in live demonstrations rather than slides, giving attendees a practical view of what an AI-augmented PM workday actually looks like. For PMs curious about Claude specifically, this is the most direct path to adoption.

## Key Takeaways
- Claude's suite of tools (Desktop, Cowork, Skills, Code) maps directly onto the key phases of PM work — discovery, planning, and execution
- Automating routine PM tasks with Claude frees up cognitive bandwidth for higher-order strategy and stakeholder work
- The barrier to getting started with Claude for PM workflows is lower than most expect — practical entry points exist today

## Who Should Read This
Product managers at any level who want a concrete, tool-specific introduction to integrating Claude into their daily workflow.

## The Brief
Pick one recurring PM task this week — a status update, a research synthesis, or a requirements draft — and run it through Claude to see the time savings firsthand.
