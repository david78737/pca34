# The Human-AI Gap: Optimizing Humans, Not Just Outputs
**Gillian Stolshek** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
This session live-demonstrates AI infrastructure built around human cognition rather than raw output efficiency, with real-time construction of personalized AI agents. Stolshek challenges the dominant framing of AI as a replacement tool and repositions it as an amplifier of human thinking. Product professionals will leave with a hands-on understanding of how to design AI workflows that make people smarter, not redundant.

## Key Takeaways
- AI infrastructure should be designed around how humans actually think, not just how fast machines can produce
- Personalized AI agents can be built and tuned in real-time to match individual cognitive styles
- The most valuable AI applications augment human judgment rather than bypass it

## Who Should Read This
Product managers and UX practitioners building or evaluating AI-powered tools who want to ensure those tools genuinely serve their users.

## The Brief
Reframe your AI strategy: stop optimizing for output volume and start designing for human cognitive performance.
