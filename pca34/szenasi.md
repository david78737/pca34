# 4 Keys to Embedding Innovation in Your Team Culture
**Scott Szenasi** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Szenasi draws on training methodologies from NASA and UnitedHealth Group to present four principles for building team cultures where innovation happens through habit rather than one-off events. The workshop focuses on collaboration, trust, and sustained behavior change — the conditions that allow new ideas to actually survive contact with organizational reality. For leaders tired of innovation initiatives that fade, this is a culture-first alternative.

## Key Takeaways
- Innovation is a cultural output, not a program — it requires embedding habits into team behavior, not just delivering content
- Trust and psychological safety are the substrate on which all other innovation practices depend
- NASA and UnitedHealth Group training principles translate directly into practical team leadership interventions

## Who Should Read This
Team leads, product directors, and people managers who want to build durable innovation capacity into their teams rather than relying on isolated brainstorming events.

## The Brief
Identify one team habit that currently suppresses innovation and replace it with a specific, repeatable behavior from this session's four principles.
