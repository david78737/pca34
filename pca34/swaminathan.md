# Product Quiz - Jeopardy Style
**Shiva Swaminathan, Harshini Mangaleshkar** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
This interactive Jeopardy-style quiz challenges product professionals on PM trends, marketing, AI, and related topics through competitive team play. It's a fun, low-stakes way to benchmark your knowledge and discover gaps across the product discipline. Prizes add an incentive to stay sharp and engaged throughout the event.

## Key Takeaways
- Gauge your current knowledge of PM, marketing, and AI trends in a competitive setting
- Identify blind spots across product topics you may not regularly encounter
- Build camaraderie with fellow attendees through collaborative team competition

## Who Should Read This
Product managers and marketers at any level who want an energizing, social way to test and reinforce their industry knowledge.

## The Brief
Show up ready to compete — this session rewards the product professionals who stay curious and broadly informed across the full spectrum of the discipline.
