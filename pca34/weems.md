# STAR Isn't Just for Interviews: Using Structured Storytelling to Drive Alignment
**Ross Weems** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Weems expands the familiar STAR (Situation-Task-Action-Result) framework beyond job interviews, applying it to PRDs, roadmap justification, stakeholder onboarding, and decision communication in enterprise B2B contexts. The session makes the case that structured storytelling is a core PM competency — not just a hiring skill — and provides templates for applying it across the most common alignment challenges. For PMs who struggle to get stakeholders on the same page, this is a communication upgrade.

## Key Takeaways
- STAR is a versatile alignment tool that improves clarity in PRDs, roadmap presentations, and stakeholder communications — not just interview answers
- Structured storytelling reduces the cognitive load on stakeholders, making complex product decisions easier to evaluate and approve
- Enterprise B2B PMs face specific alignment challenges that STAR-based communication is particularly well-suited to address

## Who Should Read This
Product managers in enterprise B2B environments who regularly present roadmaps, write PRDs, or onboard stakeholders and want to improve their clarity and alignment success rate.

## The Brief
Rewrite the intro of your next PRD or roadmap presentation using the STAR structure and measure whether stakeholder questions shift from "what does this mean" to "how do we prioritize this."
