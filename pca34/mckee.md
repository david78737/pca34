# Your Roadmap Heard It Through the Grapevine
**Jake McKee** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
McKee introduces the concept of "Conversation Debt" — the accumulated cost of building product decisions on secondhand signals instead of direct customer conversations. The hands-on workshop teaches teams how to evaluate the quality of their customer input and commit to richer, more direct conversations before each sprint. For teams that rely heavily on analytics and internal assumptions, this is a corrective intervention.

## Key Takeaways
- Conversation Debt accrues silently and distorts roadmaps — most teams have more of it than they realize
- Evaluating the quality and recency of your customer conversations is the first step to reducing it
- Committing to direct customer conversations before each sprint is a discipline that pays compound returns in roadmap accuracy

## Who Should Read This
Product managers and team leads whose roadmap decisions are driven primarily by data dashboards and internal stakeholder input rather than direct customer dialogue.

## The Brief
Before your next sprint planning, audit how much of your roadmap is based on direct customer conversations versus secondhand signals — then commit to closing the gap.
