# How I Vibe Coded the Voting & Scheduling App for ProductCamp Austin
**Tom Evans** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Evans shares the real story of building the session voting and scheduling app used at ProductCamp Austin itself — a live, high-stakes case study in vibe coding under deadline pressure. The session teaches PMs the practical mechanics of problem framing, quick iteration, and knowing when to stop building. For PMs who think coding is outside their lane, this demonstrates that meaningful tools are now within reach.

## Key Takeaways
- Vibe coding is a real, reproducible methodology — not just a buzzword — and it starts with disciplined problem framing
- Quick iteration with AI coding tools requires PMs to develop new instincts about when a prototype is "good enough" versus over-engineered
- Building the tool that ran ProductCamp's own voting is proof that PMs can ship functional, production-adjacent software with modern AI assistance

## Who Should Read This
Product managers who want to understand vibe coding from a practitioner's perspective and are curious whether they could build lightweight tools for their own teams or workflows.

## The Brief
Pick the smallest, most painful internal tool your team lacks and try vibe coding a rough version of it this week — the process will teach you more than any tutorial.
