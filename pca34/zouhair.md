# From ROI to IOR: Aligning Inputs, Outputs, and Results for Product Managers in the AI Era
**Ahmed Zouhair** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Zouhair introduces the IOR (Inputs-Outputs-Results) Framework as a more actionable alternative to traditional ROI thinking for product managers navigating AI-driven environments. The framework helps PMs connect product investments and team execution directly to measurable business outcomes. As AI reshapes how products are built and measured, IOR gives PMs a performance language that resonates with both engineers and executives.

## Key Takeaways
- ROI thinking is often too lagging and financial-centric to guide day-to-day PM decision-making in AI contexts
- The IOR Framework creates explicit linkage between what teams invest, what they produce, and what results follow
- Aligning inputs and outputs to results improves both team accountability and executive communication

## Who Should Read This
Product managers at growth-stage or enterprise companies who want a clearer framework for demonstrating and driving impact in AI-accelerated product environments.

## The Brief
Adopt IOR as your internal performance model — it will sharpen your prioritization decisions and make your impact undeniable to stakeholders.
