# From 3 Agents to Agentic Enterprise: How to Lead AI Transformation Journey
**Igor Bobryshev** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Bobryshev introduces SoftServe's Agentic Catalyst framework for scaling from isolated AI experiments to coordinated autonomous agent systems across an enterprise. The session addresses the governance and control challenges that emerge as agent complexity grows — the part most AI transformation guides skip. For product leaders tasked with enterprise AI strategy, this is a structured path from pilot chaos to organizational capability.

## Key Takeaways
- Moving from isolated AI pilots to an agentic enterprise requires deliberate architectural and governance decisions, not just more agents
- The Agentic Catalyst framework provides a phased model for coordinating autonomous systems at scale
- Control and governance aren't constraints on AI transformation — they're what makes it sustainable

## Who Should Read This
Product and technology leaders at mid-to-large organizations who are past the AI pilot phase and need a framework for scaling agent systems responsibly.

## The Brief
Assess where your organization sits on the pilot-to-enterprise spectrum and use the Agentic Catalyst framework to identify the one governance gap most likely to derail your next phase of AI scaling.
