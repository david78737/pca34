# Building a Software Startup in 7 Days from 0 to Monetization
**Matt McGuire** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
McGuire shares the unfiltered story of building and monetizing BogeyBattle.com during Masters Week 2026 — from zero to paying customers in seven days. The case study covers both what worked and what failed, giving attendees a realistic view of rapid product development under real constraints. For PMs curious about solo building or startup speed, this is a candid benchmark.

## Key Takeaways
- A software product can go from idea to monetization in seven days with focused execution and modern tooling
- Honest post-mortems of rapid builds reveal pattern failures that are highly avoidable with better upfront framing
- The Masters Week constraint forced prioritization decisions that most products never face — and the lessons transfer broadly

## Who Should Read This
Aspiring solo founders and entrepreneurially-minded PMs who want a realistic, unvarnished view of what rapid product development actually looks like end-to-end.

## The Brief
The next time you say "we need six months to build this," ask yourself what version of it you could ship in seven days — and whether that version might be enough.
