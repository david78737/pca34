# How Product Marketers Can Avoid AI-washing
**Gina Rosenthal** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Rosenthal cuts through the hype by plainly explaining AI fundamentals — including the distinction between narrow and general AI and how machine learning fits into the picture. The session equips product marketers with the vocabulary and critical eye needed to spot AI-washing in their own messaging and competitors'. In an era where "AI-powered" is slapped on everything, this talk is a practical credibility safeguard.

## Key Takeaways
- Understand the difference between narrow AI, general AI, and machine learning to communicate accurately
- Recognize the markers of AI-washing so you can avoid it in your own product claims
- Apply practical guidelines for honest, compelling AI messaging that builds rather than erodes trust

## Who Should Read This
Product marketers and PMMs responsible for positioning AI-enabled products who want their messaging to be both persuasive and defensible.

## The Brief
Before your next AI feature launch, audit your messaging against these fundamentals — your credibility with technical buyers depends on it.
