# Leading From Where You Are
**Paul Young** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Young presents a framework for understanding and developing leadership personas that applies regardless of where someone sits in an organizational hierarchy. The session provides actionable steps for increasing leadership capacity that don't require a title change or tenure milestone. For product professionals who feel their influence is limited by their role, this is a practical reframe of what leadership actually requires.

## Key Takeaways
- Leadership is a set of behaviors and capacities, not a position — it can be practiced at any career stage
- Understanding your current leadership persona is the necessary first step to deliberate growth
- Specific, actionable steps exist for expanding your leadership capacity from wherever you are today

## Who Should Read This
Early- to mid-career product managers who want to grow their organizational influence and leadership presence without waiting for a promotion.

## The Brief
Stop waiting for the title — identify your current leadership persona this week and take one concrete step to expand your capacity.
