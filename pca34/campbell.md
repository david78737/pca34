# Shipping on Someone Else's Roadmap: Lessons from Platform Ecosystems
**Katie Campbell** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Campbell draws on real Salesforce ecosystem experience to address one of the most underserved challenges in product management: building a great product when you don't control the platform it runs on. The case study covers dependency strategies, adoption hurdles, and the mindset shifts required when your roadmap is perpetually subject to someone else's decisions. For ecosystem PMs, this is rare, hard-won tactical guidance.

## Key Takeaways
- Platform dependency is a product risk that requires proactive strategy, not just reactive adaptation
- Adoption challenges in ecosystem products are distinct from those in standalone products and require different solutions
- Managing stakeholder expectations when roadmap control is limited demands a different communication playbook

## Who Should Read This
Product managers building on top of platforms like Salesforce, Shopify, or similar ecosystems who need strategies for thriving without full roadmap autonomy.

## The Brief
Map your platform dependencies today and build a contingency strategy for each — don't wait for a breaking change to discover you have no plan.
