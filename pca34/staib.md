# Double Referrals By Improving Your Customer Experience
**Karl Staib** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Staib presents the Magnetic Momentum Method — a framework for building customer trust and loyalty by systematically improving the customer journey. The session integrates AI tools for insight generation and personalization while emphasizing that human connection remains the irreplaceable element of referral-generating experiences. For product and CX professionals, this is a practical playbook for turning satisfied customers into active advocates.

## Key Takeaways
- Referral generation is a predictable outcome of deliberate customer journey design, not a happy accident
- AI can accelerate insight gathering and personalization across the customer journey without replacing the human moments that drive loyalty
- The Magnetic Momentum Method provides a step-by-step approach to identifying and fixing the friction points that suppress referrals

## Who Should Read This
Product managers, customer success leaders, and CX practitioners who want to directly connect their experience improvements to measurable referral growth.

## The Brief
Audit one stage of your customer journey this week and identify the single friction point most likely to suppress referrals — then fix it before optimizing anything else.
