# How Personalized AI Can Transform Your First 90 Days on the Job
**Jon Ulloa** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Ulloa presents original research on neurodiverse employee onboarding and a personalized AI job coach prototype designed to help people with different cognitive styles ramp faster and more confidently. The session challenges one-size-fits-all onboarding assumptions and demonstrates how AI can adapt to individual learning profiles in real-time. For product professionals in HR tech, people ops, or inclusive design, this is a compelling glimpse at the future of onboarding.

## Key Takeaways
- Standard onboarding processes fail neurodiverse employees at a disproportionate rate — and most organizations don't know it
- Personalized AI coaching can adapt to cognitive differences in ways that static documentation and cohort training cannot
- The prototype demonstrates a practical, buildable model that product teams can apply to their own onboarding or user education challenges

## Who Should Read This
Product managers in HR tech, learning and development, or enterprise SaaS who want to explore how AI personalization can improve onboarding outcomes for diverse user populations.

## The Brief
Challenge your current onboarding or user education design by asking how well it serves someone who learns fundamentally differently from the person who designed it — then explore one AI-powered adaptation.
