# From Big Tech (Amazon) to an AI Native Startup (Mia): Lessons I Learned Thus Far
**Nick Ivanecky** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Ivanecky contrasts the structured PM playbooks of Amazon with the fluid, task-based reality of operating at an AI-native startup, drawing on direct experience at both. The session covers AI tool selection, the redefined relationship between PMs and engineering in AI-native environments, and what enterprise habits are worth unlearning. For PMs considering a move to a startup or AI-native company, this is an honest orientation.

## Key Takeaways
- Enterprise PM frameworks provide a solid foundation but several core assumptions break down in AI-native startup contexts
- Task-based work and compressed decision cycles require PMs to develop new operating instincts beyond what big tech teaches
- The PM-engineering-design relationship is fundamentally different at AI-native companies — adapting quickly is a competitive advantage

## Who Should Read This
Mid-to-senior PMs at large tech companies who are curious about or planning a transition to startup or AI-native environments.

## The Brief
List the three enterprise PM habits you rely on most and honestly assess which ones would help, which would hurt, and which would be irrelevant at an AI-native startup.
