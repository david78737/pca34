# The 30 Minute Hackathon: Redefining the Product Process with Vibe Coding
**JD Piquard** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
Piquard demonstrates a compressed, end-to-end product workflow — from user research to functional prototype — completed in under 30 minutes using Claude Code and platforms like Replit and Bolt. The session reframes vibe coding not as a shortcut but as a legitimate discovery and handoff methodology. For PMs tired of slow prototyping cycles, this is a glimpse at what rapid iteration looks like when AI does the heavy lifting.

## Key Takeaways
- A complete product workflow from research to prototype is achievable in 30 minutes with the right AI toolchain
- Claude Code paired with Replit or Bolt enables non-engineers to produce functional prototypes ready for engineering review
- Engineering handoff becomes faster and clearer when prototypes are generated from structured user research inputs

## Who Should Read This
Product managers and designers who want to dramatically compress their discovery-to-prototype cycle and communicate requirements through working code rather than static docs.

## The Brief
Run a 30-minute vibe coding sprint on your next feature idea before writing a single PRD — you'll validate faster and hand off cleaner.
