# From Enterprise PM to Solo Founder: What Translates, What Doesn't, and What Drove First Revenue
**Russ Sadrieff** · PCA34 · April 18, 2026

## Synopsis
With ten years at Dell and Visa behind him, Sadrieff offers a candid assessment of which enterprise PM skills carry over to solo founding and which ones actively get in the way. The session covers the specific frameworks that accelerated his path to first revenue and the enterprise practices he had to unlearn to move fast enough. For experienced PMs eyeing a founder path, this is a rare, experience-grounded map of the transition.

## Key Takeaways
- Several core enterprise PM competencies — structured discovery, stakeholder communication, prioritization frameworks — transfer directly to solo founding
- Enterprise habits around consensus-building, process adherence, and risk aversion can significantly slow a solo founder's path to validation
- First revenue is a learnable outcome — specific, translatable approaches exist for reaching it faster than most enterprise PMs expect

## Who Should Read This
Senior product managers with enterprise backgrounds who are seriously considering or actively planning a transition to solo founding or early-stage startup roles.

## The Brief
Map your enterprise PM skills into three buckets — keep, adapt, and unlearn — and let that map guide your first 90 days if you make the leap.
